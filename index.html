iv.

Balang araw,
we used to say

Someday we'll have our own car. Someday our own couch. Someday a home with our names on it.

From lying on a borrowed floor to standing in front of our own home.

Look how far we've come, love. Someday
is today. and I'd do it all again, with you

My love,

Every photo here was once an ordinary day. We didn't know back then that we were building something. We were just tired, and broke, and laughing, and holding on.

But look at it now. The little home with the Christmas lights. The dogs waiting at the door. The car we used to dream about. All of it started with you choosing me, again and again, on the days that were easy and the days that weren't.

Thank you for staying. Thank you for believing in our "balang araw" even when it felt so far away.

We're not done yet. There are so many more somedays left, and I want every single one of them with you.

Forever yours

I love you.

Still choosing you. Every single day.
